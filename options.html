Suspending tabs that are still loading

Stop tabs mid-load

When you suspend a big batch of tabs, Chrome has only started loading a few of them — the rest are queued and cannot be suspended the normal way. With this on, TabRest stops those tabs at once and rests them on a small TabRest page that remembers where each one was going. Click the tab to go back to the real page.

Turn this off and queued tabs are left to finish loading first, then suspended. Nothing is stopped early and no tab ever shows a TabRest address — but a large batch keeps downloading for a while after you press the button.

Worth knowing: a tab resting on the TabRest page needs TabRest installed to return on its own. If you remove the extension while tabs are resting there, their addresses are still readable in the address bar, but you would have to reopen them yourself.
